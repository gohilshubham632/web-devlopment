gujrat

 Gujarat is a beautiful state in western India. Its capital is Gandhinagar, and its largest city is Ahmedabad. Gujarat is famous for its rich culture, traditional dances, and delicious food. Garba and Dandiya are popular folk dances. The Gir National Park is the home of Asiatic lions. The Statue of Unity, dedicated to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, is a famous tourist attraction. Gujarat is also known for its industries, textiles, and diamond business. The people of Gujarat celebrate festivals like Navratri and Uttarayan with great joy. Gujarati is the main language spoken in this wonderful state of India.

Rajashan

Rajasthan is a beautiful state in the northwestern part of India. Its capital is Jaipur, which is also known as the Pink City. Rajasthan is famous for its deserts, forts, palaces, and rich culture. The Thar Desert is one of its major attractions. The state is home to famous places like Hawa Mahal, Amber Fort, and Udaipur City Palace. People wear colorful traditional clothes and enjoy folk dances and music. Dal Baati Churma is a popular dish of Rajasthan. The state celebrates festivals like Teej and Gangaur with great enthusiasm. Rajasthan attracts many tourists from India and abroad every year.
